# Result tables

Small tab-separated tables written by the stages and read by the figure scripts and the manuscript. Large outputs stay
in `work/`.

| Table | Written by | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `stage1_*.tsv` | `make stage1` (`stage1/collect.py`) | stage 1 outputs of the pipeline: genes, selenocysteine sites, annotation classes, tRNA-Sec, machinery, RF00031 scores |
| `stage1_secis_scores*.tsv`, `stage1_rf00031_scores.tsv` | `make secis-stage1`, `make stage1-figures` | RF00031 scores of 3′ sequences and random windows |
| `baseline_vs_stage1*.tsv` | `make curation` | curated *O. dioica* baseline against stage 1 |
| `stage2_secis_model_rounds.tsv`, `stage2_secis_scores.tsv` | `make secis-model`, `make secis-stage2` | appendicularian SECIS model: bootstrap rounds and scores |
| `stage2_machinery_profiles_test.tsv` | `make machinery-test` | appendicularian machinery profiles against stage 1 |
| `stage2_machinery_fragments.tsv` | `make machinery-fragments` | PSTK fragments discarded by Selenoprofiles |
| `stage2_machinery_census.{tsv,md}` | `make machinery-census` | machinery per gene and genome |
| `stage2_absence_{pstk,secp43}.*.tsv` | `make machinery-absence` | absence tests: candidates, controls, summary |
| `stage2_machinery_rbh.tsv` (`stage1_machinery_rbh.tsv` when regenerated) | `make machinery-rbh` | reciprocal best hits against the human proteome |
| `stage2_machinery_lba.tsv` | `make machinery-lba` | supports and AU tests of the machinery trees, all families |
| `stage2_machinery_domains.tsv`, `stage2_machinery_domain_conflicts.tsv` | `make machinery-domains` | domain analysis of the machinery trees: supports and AU tests per domain; strongly supported conflicts between the two domain trees |
| `stage2_machinery_domains_pmsf.tsv`, `stage2_machinery_domain_conflicts_pmsf.tsv` | `make machinery-domains-pmsf` | the same under LG+C60+F+G (PMSF) |
| `stage2_machinery_table.{md,tsv}` | `make machinery-table` | Table MACHINERY of the manuscript: census with orthology evidence |
| `stage2_selenoprotein_rbh.tsv` | `make selenoprotein-rbh` | reciprocal best hits of the stage 1 selenoprotein predictions against the human proteome |
| `stage2_selenoprotein_lba.tsv` | `make selenoprotein-pmsf` | supports of the largest appendicularian clade in the selenoprotein trees, maximum likelihood and PMSF |
| `stage2_selenoprotein_orthology.{tsv,md}` | `make selenoprotein-orthology` | first-pass orthology of each appendicularian selenoprotein clade |
| `stage2_secis_hits.tsv`, `stage2_secis_hits_summary.tsv`, `stage2_secis_hits_families.tsv` | `make secis-hits` | step 1: validated selenocysteine hits with their RF00031 and current-model SECIS scores; per genome and per family |
| `stage2_secis_v2_model_seeds.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_model_identities.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_model_positions.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_model_pairs.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_model_orthologs.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_model_summary.tsv` | `make secis-description` | description of the Oikopleura SECIS model: seeds (family, position after the stop codon, nearest seed, group, motifs, canonical pairs per helix) and the SECIS hits of TXNL1 and KKSU; identity between each two seeds; residue counts per consensus position; per base pair, canonical pairs and mutual information in the seeds and in decoys (best hits in shuffled windows); changes between the orthologous SECIS of the three O. dioica assemblies per base pair; summary with the tests |
| `stage2_secis_orphan_hits.tsv` | `make secis-orphans` | genome-scan hits at the genome threshold without a selenoprotein gene upstream: annotation at the hit and upstream, best blastx hit of the 5 kb upstream (human RefSeq and gold-standard proteins) with the stop codons in the alignment, copies in the genome, conservation in the other Oikopleura assemblies |
| `stage2_txnl1_paralogs_queries.tsv`, `_sites.tsv`, `_proteins.tsv`, `_tree.tsv`, `_neighbours.tsv` | `make txnl1-paralogs` | the full-length TXNL1 and the selenocysteine copy of O. dioica: residue aligned to the selenocysteine in each copy and in human TXNL1; their sites in other genomes (structure, codon at the selenocysteine position, codons to the stop, SECIS, annotation, best human gene, PITH-region sequence); their hits in the proteomes; the sister group and support of the O. dioica copies in the PITH tree; the neighbourhood of the full-length copy (GPR107-like gene, merged models, transcripts spanning both) |
| `stage2_sec_codon_positions.tsv`, `stage2_sec_codon_positions_summary.tsv` | `make sec-positions` | position of the selenocysteine codon in each gold-standard model (exon, distance to junctions, codons to the stop) and its rank among the sense codons of the gene; summary for OSKA2016 (all genes, one per family) against uniform positions |
| `stage2_tandem_stops_codons.tsv`, `_secis.tsv`, `_candidates.tsv` | `make tandem-stops` | per annotated genome: primary stop codons and the next in-frame stop (+1 to +3) against the background frequency; SECIS-like hits (genome scan of step 4 and its shuffled copy) in the 2 kb after the stop codon by class of gene end; genes ending with TGA and a stop at +1 or +2 with a hit at the genome threshold |
| `stage2_txnl1_introns.tsv` | `make txnl1-introns` | coding introns of TXNL1.b and TXNL1.a in the three O. dioica assemblies: residue after the intron, phase, position on TXNL1.a, shared or not |
| `stage2_txnl1_homologs.tsv`, `stage2_txnl1_homologs_summary.tsv` | `make txnl1-homologs` | O. dioica TXNL1 against human proteins (forward); human TXNL1, its thioredoxin part and its PITH part against the proteins and genomes of the three O. dioica assemblies (reverse); O. dioica TXNL1 against its own assembly (self); for each top subject, position relative to the TXNL1 locus, annotation and best human gene; summary of the answers per assembly |
| `stage2_secis_scan_classes.tsv` | `make secis-description` | genome-scan hits at the genome threshold, classed by what lies upstream on the same strand: `class`, from the screens (selenoprotein gene, copy of a known selenoprotein, KKSU, stage 1 prediction without selenocysteine, TGA pair shared with another species, nothing found; input of `make secis-orphans`); `final_class` and `evidence`, refined with `stage2_secis_orphan_hits.tsv` (copies of known selenoproteins found by blastx or by alignment to the hit of a selenoprotein gene in another assembly; repeated sequences) |
| `stage2_secis_v2_windows.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_rounds.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_summary.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_heldout.tsv`, `stage2_secis_v2_negatives.tsv` | `make secis-v2` | step 3: positive and cysteine windows; bootstrap rounds per fold; per fold and model, held-out windows detected and negatives above threshold, with pooled rows; held-out scores of each positive window; evaluation negatives above the training threshold, with coordinates |
| `stage2_secis_scan_summary.tsv`, `stage2_secis_scan_hits.tsv` | `make secis-scan`, `make uga-screen` | step 4: SECIS hits of the genome-wide scans (Oikopleura model) per genome (including the held-out B. stygius, M. erythrocephalus and F. borealis), real and window-shuffled, by score, and the genome-scan threshold; hits at or above the threshold, with the known gene upstream and the candidates of the comparative screen |
| `stage2_uga_screen.tsv`, `stage2_uga_screen_summary.tsv` | `make uga-screen` | step 4: UGA/SECIS candidates of the transcript screen (O. dioica annotations and transcript assemblies; human and C. intestinalis controls) and of the comparative screen (TGA/TGA pairs between species upstream of genome-scan hits), with known genes, best human and gold-standard hits and the assemblies sharing the candidate; counts per genome and source |
| `stage2_candidates_rule.tsv`, `stage2_candidates_genomes.tsv`, `stage2_candidates_proteomes.tsv` | `make candidate-genes` | step 5: the rule fixed in advance for the candidate genes in the three O. dioica assemblies (codon, context, SECIS after the stop codon, verdict); tblastn of the candidate proteins against the appendicularian, tunicate and amphioxus genomes (best locus, codon at the selenocysteine position, codons to the next stop, SECIS after it with the Oikopleura model and RF00031); blastp against RefSeq proteomes (residue aligned to the selenocysteine, length after it) |
| `gold_standard.tsv` | `make gold-standard` | step 2: gold-standard gene model of each curated *O. dioica* selenoprotein gene: evidence (transcript, annotated mRNA or prediction), protein source (assembly or transcript, with identities to the other assemblies), completeness, introns and non-canonical splice sites, UTRs, differences between transcripts and assembly, public annotation status and the change it needs |
| `stage2_profile_members.tsv` | `make stage2-inputs` | steps 6 and 7: candidate members of each stage 2 profile build (E0, LOO_OKI, LOO_OSKA, LOO_OdB3, final): source (gold, stage1, validated, curated), genome, kept or the reason not, residues left in the profile alignment |
| `stage2_truth_sets.tsv` | `make stage2-truth` | steps 6 and 7: reference sets of the benchmark: gold-standard models, gold models lifted to KUM, AOM and Bar2 (status of each lifted model, identity to the source gold protein, other copies), validated stage 1 predictions and KKSU sites of the other *Oikopleura* species; codon by its lowest coordinate |
| `stage2_benchmark_genes.tsv`, `stage2_benchmark_summary.tsv`, `stage2_benchmark_other_sites.tsv` | `make stage2-benchmark` | step 7: each pipeline run (stage 1, stage 2 runs) against the reference sets: per gene (Sec site by Selenoprofiles or miniprot, family, locus when the codon is missed, introns, coding-base overlap, SECIS), per run and genome, and Sec sites outside the reference sets |
